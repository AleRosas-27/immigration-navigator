# Immigration Navigator

A RAG-powered assistant that helps international students navigate the
F-1 → OPT → STEM OPT → H-1B pipeline. Every answer is grounded in official
USCIS sources with citations.

UC Berkeley MIDS Capstone, Summer 2026 — Alejandra Rosas, Clover Ausdemore,
Duc Nguyen, Rohan.

Not legal advice. Consult your school's international student office or an
immigration attorney for decisions about your status.
